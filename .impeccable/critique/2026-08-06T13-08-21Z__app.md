## Design Specificity Verdict

**Authored — but one lane from the default.**

The palette derives from the product's material world: `madder` is a natural dye used in South Asian textiles; `linen`/`sand`/`parchment`/`walnut`/`umber` are fiber and pigment vocabulary. Fraunces + Jost is a deliberate pairing. COD-first framing, the `"Ayesha Khan"` / `"0300 1234567"` / `"House 12, Street 4, F-8/1"` placeholders, and "our rider collects payment at your door" are product truth no template produces.

Caveat: warm cream ground (`#faf6ef`) + high-contrast serif display + earthy accent is precisely the most common AI-generated cluster. It lands adjacent to the default and earns it through visibly textile-derived reasoning — but it is one decision away from reading as generated.

Specificity thins in **layout**, not color. Eyebrow → display heading → centered section header, repeated down the homepage, is interchangeable e-commerce rhythm. The most product-specific composition is the size-chart block rendering the real charts rather than a picture of them — and it is buried last before the footer.

**Deterministic scan:** `detect.mjs` over `app/` + `components/` returned `[]`, exit 0. No slop signatures. Detector reads HTML/CSS and this is Tailwind-in-JSX, so treat as "no obvious signatures" rather than full absolution; manual read agrees.

**Visual overlays:** not attempted — no dev server running, no browser automation exposed. All findings are source-derived.

## Overall Impression

Well above average, and the code knows why it does things — comments explaining the `backdrop-filter` containing-block trap, the hero pills' `pointer-events` bug, and why `prefetch={false}` "read as the site is broken" are notes most codebases never write down.

Biggest opportunity: **accessibility is the one dimension where intent and execution diverge.** Clear a11y effort throughout — `aria-label` on every icon button, `aria-live` on stock changes, `inert` on hidden slides, `sr-only` h1 fallback — but the color system fails WCAG AA in measurable places and there is essentially no reduced-motion story. Considered semantically, skipped visually.

## What's Working

1. **Complete token system.** 15 semantic colors in `@theme`; zero hard-coded hex in components.
2. **Honest responsive tables.** `orders-table.tsx:95` drops to a card list on phones — "a six-column table can't honestly fit 400px" — instead of a horizontal-scroll shrug.
3. **Empty states do their job.** "No orders yet — they'll appear here the moment a customer checks out"; "All variants comfortably stocked."
4. **Correct sanitization architecture.** Rich text sanitized server-side on save (`lib/sanitize.ts` via `app/actions/admin.ts:153`), so the eight `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` sites are safe.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Placeholder and disabled-state text fails WCAG AA
`placeholder:text-umber/50` on `bg-white/70` computes to **2.18:1** against 4.5:1 required. Every checkout input. Same value styles out-of-stock sizes in `variant-picker.tsx:127`, where it carries meaning rather than hint text.
**Why it matters:** Placeholders do instructional work here ("House 12, Street 4, F-8/1" teaches address format). Invisible out-of-stock sizes cause wasted taps at the buying moment.
**Fix:** Raise to the minimum passing alpha; give out-of-stock a treatment that is not opacity alone.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable audit`

### [P1] No reduced-motion support
`globals.css` defines nine animations — fades, slides, pops, a 1.6s infinite shimmer, an infinite nav-progress bar — and contains no `prefers-reduced-motion` block. Only honoring in the codebase is `hero-slideshow.tsx:35` (stops autoplay).
**Why it matters:** Full-bleed hero with 1100ms crossfades, infinite shimmer, and card hover-scale is the profile that triggers vestibular symptoms. WCAG 2.3.3.
**Fix:** Add the media block; keep opacity fades, drop transforms and infinite loops.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable animate`

### [P1] `Dialog` claims modality it does not implement
`dialog.tsx:52` sets `aria-modal="true"` with no focus trap, no initial focus, no focus restore.
**Why it matters:** Keyboard/screen-reader users tab out of the cart drawer into the page behind while still announced as inside a modal; on close, focus lands at document top. WCAG 2.4.3.
**Fix:** Focus on open, trap Tab, restore to trigger on close. One fix covers cart drawer, size-chart modal, and every admin dialog.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

### [P1] Custom pill controls have no visible focus state
`Button` has `focus-visible:outline-2 focus-visible:outline-walnut`; hand-rolled buttons do not — colour/size pills (`variant-picker.tsx:86`, `:118`), qty stepper, hero dots and arrows, checkout payment-method cards.
**Why it matters:** The payment selector is a keyboard user choosing between COD and bank transfer with no indication of focus, immediately before spending money.
**Fix:** Lift the `focus-visible` ring into a shared class; apply to every interactive element.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

### [P2] Ten native `confirm()` calls break the design system
`admin-nav.tsx:155`, `product-form.tsx:170`, `order-controls.tsx:41`, and seven more — including "Delete "X"? This can't be undone."
**Why it matters:** Unstylable OS chrome in a carefully built ivory/walnut world; a jarring system sheet on mobile Safari. Weak protection for irreversible deletion — a reflexive Enter dismisses it. A `Dialog` component already exists.
**Fix:** `ConfirmDialog` built on `Dialog`; type-to-confirm for irreversible actions.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey (distracted mobile user)** — primary persona; most traffic is phone.
- **Hero dots are 8px tall** (`hero-slideshow.tsx:219`, `h-2`, no padding) — the main carousel control at roughly a fifth of the 44px minimum. Thumb-misses, or slides 2–4 never seen.
- **No persistent pause control.** Autoplay pauses on touch, resumes on release; slide 2 swaps out mid-read at 6.5s. WCAG 2.2.2.
- **Checkout has no draft persistence.** Six fields filled, a WhatsApp interruption, and React `useState` is gone. Most expensive abandonment in the app.
- Positive: `-mx-4 snap-x` on the trending row is a genuinely good mobile pattern; quick-add stays visible on touch instead of hiding behind hover.

**Sam (screen reader / keyboard)**
- **Nested interactive elements.** `CardAddButton` renders `<button>` inside the card's `<Link>` — acknowledged at `card-add-button.tsx:13`. `<a>` cannot legally contain a button; announcement is inconsistent and keyboard traversal unpredictable.
- Tabs out of the cart drawer into the page behind it.
- Positive: `inert` + `aria-hidden` on inactive slides is the correct modern solution; `aria-live="polite"` on stock state is exactly right.

**Jordan (first-timer)**
- Well served. COD explained three times in plain language before commitment; Size Guide top-level; bank-transfer instructions spell out what to screenshot.
- Snag: `"Something went wrong."` tells Jordan nothing about whether the order was placed.

### Systemic patterns

1. **Opacity used as a design token.** `/50`, `/40`, `/35` modifiers appear throughout as lightening shorthand; every failing contrast value is an alpha modifier. Fix is systemic — explicit `--color-*-muted` tokens with verified ratios.
2. **Touch targets cluster at 32–36px.** `Button size="sm"` is `h-8`; `Dialog` close is 32px; header actions 36px; hero dots 8px. 44px is not met outside `size="lg"`.
3. **`focus-visible` exists in exactly one component.**

### Positive technical findings

- Every `next/image` carries correct `sizes`, `priority` only above-fold, `fill` with explicit aspect containers. Hero videos use `preload="metadata"` for non-first slides and pause when not visible.
- The prefetch decision is documented with its failure history (`product-card.tsx:39`).
- No dark mode is the right call — a fashion storefront committed to a single warm world does not need one.
- `min-w-0` discipline on flex/grid children applied deliberately, with comments.

## Questions to Consider

- The size-chart block is the most product-specific composition and it closes the page. What if the homepage led with fit confidence? Fit is the top reason online clothing is returned.
- What would the hero look like if it were not a carousel? Slides 2–4 typically get single-digit engagement.
- Is there a version of this palette that keeps the textile logic but steps away from the cream-and-serif center of gravity?
